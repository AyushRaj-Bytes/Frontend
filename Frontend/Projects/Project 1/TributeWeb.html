A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

1931 - 2015

Missile Man Of India

A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was a distinguished Indian scientist and statesman who served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007. Popularly known as the "Missile Man of India," he played a crucial role in the development of India’s space and missile technology, including the Agni and Prithvi missiles. Kalam was deeply involved with ISRO and DRDO, where his leadership helped strengthen India’s defense and scientific capabilities. Despite his remarkable achievements, he was known for his simple lifestyle, humility, and strong connection with students. He believed that education, innovation, and dreaming big were the keys to national development, and his life continues to inspire people across the world. Beyond his scientific achievements, he was admired for his humility, wisdom, and dedication to inspiring young minds. His life and writings continue to motivate millions to dream big and work hard for the nation.
